Known issues and limits

Confirmed product defects are tracked openly with the known issue label. Review those reports and the current operating limits before filing a duplicate.

View open known issuesBrowse all issues

Current operating limits

  • Community supports up to 25,000 input events.
  • Professional has a measured 100,000-event baseline. Adaptive Scale may attempt larger complete selections when local Power BI resources permit, up to an absolute ceiling of 1,000,000 events.
  • Larger capacity is not guaranteed and varies with data shape, memory, Power BI, WebView2, and device resources.
  • Threadseer never mines an incomplete prefix after resource admission stops loading data.
  • Changed fields or filters can make the current snapshot stale; use Reanalyze deliberately to replace it.

Before reporting a problem

Follow the support checklist and reproduce the behavior with synthetic data. Do not attach operational or personal data to a public issue.
