Verified public changes to Threadseer will be recorded here by version and release date.

No verified public Marketplace release yet. The Microsoft Marketplace approval process is in progress. Current Threadseer 0.1.x packages are approval candidates, not public Marketplace releases, and there is no verified download or purchase link to publish yet.

0.1.x validation series

Public release date: Not released · Last reviewed: August 10, 2026

Available in current approval candidates

  • Explicit Analyze and Reanalyze snapshot lifecycle.
  • Process discovery across Process Map, Variants, Compare, Bottlenecks, Rework, Improve, Cases, and Data Quality.
  • Side-by-side variant comparison plus bounded workflow-regularity, resource/handoff, and shortcut evidence in Compare and Improve.
  • Case-level traceability, Power BI interactions, quality disclosure, cancellation, and stale-snapshot handling.
  • Community and Professional edition policy with Microsoft-host licensing integration.
  • Empty external network privileges and no publisher-operated process telemetry.

Public availability

Threadseer is currently in the Microsoft Marketplace approval process. The reserved offer name is Threadseer; the initial market is the United States; Community is free; and the approved Professional launch price is US$20 per assigned user per month or US$200 per assigned user per year. There is no time-limited Professional trial at launch because Community is the permanent free evaluation path. Public installation, purchasing, assignment, version, and Marketplace-link details will be published here only after approval and go-live are verified. Power BI certification is deferred until after Marketplace launch.
