# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue. Submit them through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/jasonwi1202/threadseer-support/security/advisories/new).

Describe the affected Threadseer version, impact, reproduction steps, and any mitigations you have identified. Do not include customer event data, credentials, tenant secrets, or other information you are not authorized to share.

Reports are reviewed on a reasonable-efforts basis. A submission does not create a response-time or remediation-time commitment.
